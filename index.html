How to Use This Tracker

Welcome to My Budget Tracker!

  1. Enter the name of your expense.
  2. Enter the amount spent in Kenyan Shillings.
  3. Select an expense category from the dropdown.
  4. Click the Add Expense button.
  5. View your expenses in the table below.
